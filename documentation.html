Ideation

Brainstorming & Early Concepts

In the ideation phase, we explored diverse concepts for bringing Eindhoven's heritage to life through AI. We facilitated collaborative brainstorming sessions, mapped ideas visually, and prioritized features using the MoSCoW method.

Research

Context, Users & Background

Our research phase focused on understanding Frits Philips' historical context, analyzing existing cultural heritage installations, and defining our target audience. We conducted literature studies, competitive analysis, and stakeholder mapping to ground our concept.

Concepting

Proof of Concept Explorations

During the concepting phase, we developed multiple POC one-pagers to explore different directions for interactive AI experiences in Eindhoven. Each concept presented a unique approach to engaging the public with city heritage through technology.

Validation & Meetings

Testing, Feedback & Stakeholder Engagement

We validated our concept through user testing at the AI & Data Event, gathered survey data, and maintained regular communication with stakeholders. This phase included standups, retrospectives, legal consultations, and iterative refinement based on feedback.

Planning

Roadmap, Milestones & Deliverables

Our planning artifacts include timelines, next-step definitions, deliverable checklists, and process documentation. These materials ensured clear ownership, milestone tracking, and structured workflow throughout the semester.
