Ideation

Brainstorming & Early Concepts

In the ideation phase, we explored diverse concepts for bringing Eindhoven's heritage to life through AI. We facilitated collaborative brainstorming sessions, mapped ideas visually, and prioritized features using the MoSCoW method.

Proof of Concept

Prototype & Feasibility

We developed multiple one-pagers and prototypes to explore feasibility for interactive AI experiences in Eindhoven. Each concept presented a unique approach to engaging the public through technology.

Working Concept

Integrated Experience Iteration

We iterated towards a cohesive, working concept by integrating findings, refining interaction flows, and establishing core behaviors of the experience.

Research

Deep Dives & Context

We expanded our understanding of Frits Philips' context and related domains, conducting deeper literature studies, competitive analyses, and stakeholder mapping to refine our direction.

Testing

User Feedback & Iteration

We validated our concept through user testing, gathered survey data, and maintained regular communication with stakeholders. This phase included standups, retrospectives, legal consultations, and iterative refinement based on feedback.

Delivery

Final Deliverables

We prepared final deliverables, documentation, and wrap-up materials, ensuring clear ownership, milestone tracking, and structured workflows throughout the semester.
