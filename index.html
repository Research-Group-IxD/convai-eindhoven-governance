Introduction

Our group project from Eindhoven AI focuses entirely on developing and exploring possibilities around bringing objects in Eindhoven to life. For this, we have chosen to create a virtual version of Frits Philips. We want to use an AI agent that has his voice, character, and knowledge, and can talk with people about Eindhoven, the city's history, and culture.

The project mainly falls within the domains of cultural heritage, urban technology, and interactive AI. By exploring whether we can attach a digital personality to a well-known figure from the city, we examine whether AI can contribute to the cultural preservation of the city.

Our future users and therefore target audience (and stakeholders) are as follows:

  • Residents of Eindhoven: Residents who want to learn more about their own city and history.
  • Tourists/(museum) visitors of Eindhoven: People who can visit an interactive installation to get to know the city.
