Introduction

Our group project from Eindhoven AI focuses entirely on developing and exploring possibilities around bringing objects in Eindhoven to life. For this, we have chosen to create a virtual version of Frits Philips. We want to use an AI agent that has his voice, character, and knowledge, and can talk with people about Eindhoven, the city's history, and culture.

The project mainly falls within the domains of cultural heritage, urban technology, and interactive AI. By exploring whether we can attach a digital personality to a well-known figure from the city, we examine whether AI can contribute to the cultural preservation of the city.

Our future users and therefore target audience (and stakeholders) are as follows:

  • Residents of Eindhoven: Residents who want to learn more about their own city and history.
  • Tourists/(museum) visitors of Eindhoven: People who can visit an interactive installation to get to know the city.

Results

The results of our research show that the concept of an AI-driven version of Frits Philips is both relevant and feasible. The literature study provided a clear understanding of Frits Philips’ personality and values, which successfully translated into a recognizable and human-like AI character. The competitive analysis revealed that many existing interactive installations lack real conversational depth, confirming that our concept fills a clear gap within this domain.

User testing at the AI & Data Event and the survey results showed that users experience the interaction with AI-Frits as realistic, pleasant, and engaging. Participants did not perceive Frits as a standard chatbot, but as a believable character with a strong connection to Eindhoven. At the same time, the results highlighted areas for improvement, such as adding more personal stories, refining language use, and shortening responses.

Overall, the findings confirm that the project has a strong foundation and clear potential. The results support our design choices and provide concrete direction for further development toward a complete and meaningful proof of concept.

Conclusion

This research shows that bringing a historical figure like Frits Philips to life through AI is both meaningful and promising within the context of cultural heritage and urban technology. By combining literature study, competitive analysis, and user testing, we demonstrated that an AI-driven character can be perceived as human, engaging, and relevant to the city of Eindhoven.

The project’s main contribution lies in exploring how conversational AI can move beyond scripted interactions and support cultural preservation in a modern, interactive way. While further work is needed to deepen the character, refine responses, and address ethical considerations, the research confirms that this concept has clear value and strong potential for future development and real-world application.
